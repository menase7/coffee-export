# Project: Premium Ethiopian Coffee Export Business Website

## 1. Project Overview

Build a premium, modern, professional, and fully responsive website for an Ethiopian coffee export company.

The company exports authentic Ethiopian coffee to international markets and wants to establish trust with international coffee importers, wholesale buyers, distributors, roasters, and hospitality businesses.

The website must communicate the company's identity, the quality and authenticity of Ethiopian coffee, its products and coffee origins, export capabilities, and commitment to reliable international business relationships.

The primary goal is to generate qualified business inquiries from international buyers and make it easy for potential partners to request quotations, discuss bulk orders, and establish long-term partnerships.

The website should look like a premium international coffee trading company, not a local coffee shop, restaurant, or generic e-commerce template.

Create a complete, polished website with thoughtful animations, beautiful Ethiopian coffee imagery, professional content, strong typography, excellent user experience, and production-minded implementation.

Company information should be configurable so the business owner can replace placeholder details with the actual company name, logo, contact information, certifications, product specifications, and export markets.

## 2. Design Direction and Brand Identity

Create a sophisticated visual identity inspired by Ethiopian coffee culture, traditional coffee ceremonies, coffee beans, roasted coffee, Ethiopian highlands, and the natural environments where specialty coffee grows.

### Visual style

Use a premium dark theme with rich, earthy colors.

Suggested color palette:

- Primary background: Deep espresso brown, #17110E
- Secondary background: Dark roasted coffee, #211712
- Card background: #2A1E17
- Primary accent: Warm coffee gold, #C99A52
- Secondary accent: Copper brown, #A9693B
- Primary text: Warm ivory, #F5EFE6
- Secondary text: Muted beige, #C2B4A4
- Borders: Subtle dark bronze, #493629
- Highlight color: Natural coffee crema, #D9B889

Use colors consistently throughout the website.

Avoid excessive gradients, bright neon colors, generic purple AI-style gradients, or unnecessary visual effects.

The website should feel warm, authentic, elegant, natural, trustworthy, and internationally competitive.

### Typography

Use an elegant serif font for major headings, such as Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond.

Use a clean sans-serif font, such as Inter or Manrope, for body text, navigation, buttons, and forms.

Use generous spacing, strong visual hierarchy, carefully balanced layouts, and readable text sizes.

### Layout

Use a combination of:

- Full-width photographic hero sections.
- Asymmetrical editorial layouts.
- Large, elegant headings.
- High-quality coffee photography.
- Minimal borders and refined cards.
- Subtle earthy textures where appropriate.
- Spacious product presentations.
- Clear calls to action.
- Carefully designed mobile layouts.

Do not make every section look like a grid of identical cards. Introduce visual variety and a clear narrative as visitors scroll through the website.

## 15. SEO Requirements

SEO must be implemented as a core part of the project, not added as an afterthought.

### Technical SEO

- Use semantic HTML5.
- Use one clear primary H1 per page.
- Organize content with logical H2 and H3 headings.
- Use descriptive, readable URLs.
- Implement page-specific title tags.
- Implement unique meta descriptions.
- Add canonical URLs using the production domain once known.
- Generate an XML sitemap.
- Configure robots.txt correctly.
- Avoid accidentally blocking important public pages from search engines.
- Add descriptive image alt attributes.
- Ensure links are crawlable and accessible.
- Avoid duplicate page titles and descriptions.
- Add breadcrumbs where appropriate.
- Use server-rendered or statically generated content where supported by the chosen framework.
- Ensure important page content is available to search engines without requiring client-side interaction.
- Implement appropriate redirects if URLs change.
- Avoid broken links and unnecessary redirect chains.

- Organization.
